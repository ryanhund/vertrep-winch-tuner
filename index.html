The side of the helicopter where the winch is located. On most ships, this also affects the direction the helicopter approaches from. A helicopter with the winch on the starboard side will approach the ship from left to right, so that the hoist operator can see the target and provide instructions to the pilot.

For helicopters with winches on the stern ramp, direction is your preference.
